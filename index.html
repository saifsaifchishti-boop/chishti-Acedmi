Frequently asked questions

Is the portal free?

Yes. Handouts, tests, the planner and the coding lab are free to use.

How are practice tests created?

Select a handout and a page range. The AI reads that text and writes MCQs, short or long questions with answers.

Where is my data stored?

Scores, plans and reviews are stored in your own browser. Clearing browser data removes them.

Can I use my own PDF?

Yes. Upload a PDF in the Test Center. It is read in your browser and is not stored on our side.

How to apply

Follow these steps to take admission. Dates, fees and eligibility are set by the university, so confirm them on the official website before paying anything.

  1. Choose a program. Review levels, duration and details under Programs.
  2. Check eligibility. Match your previous qualification and marks with the program requirement.
  3. Prepare documents. Keep scanned copies of your national ID (CNIC or B-Form), academic certificates and a recent photograph.
  4. Apply online. Complete the application on the official admission website.
  5. Pay the admission fee. Use only the payment methods listed on the official website, and keep the receipt.
  6. Receive your student ID and sign in. Open the official LMS to start your courses.

Guidelines

  • Apply only through the official website. This portal is an independent study-support service.
  • Never share your LMS password with anyone.
  • Keep your application and fee receipts until admission is confirmed.
  • Follow the academic calendar and submission deadlines shown on the LMS.
  • Contact the administrator for help with study material.
